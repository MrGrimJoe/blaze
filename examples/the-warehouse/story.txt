TITLE: The Warehouse
SUBTITLE: a very short mystery
STYLE: bubbles
CAST: JOHN = man, detective, red coat, glasses
CAST: MARY = woman, blue dress, long hair
CAST: TIM = boy, green hoodie, cap

SCENE: rainy street at night
JOHN: Did you hear that? Something moved inside the warehouse.
MARY: It's just the wind, John. Let's go home.
[CAMERA push]
JOHN: Wind doesn't knock over boxes.
[WEATHER storm]
> Thunder rolled across the empty street.
MARY: Fine. But you go first.
[MOVE JOHN right]

INT. WAREHOUSE - NIGHT
JOHN: Hello? Is anyone in here?
[PAUSE 0.6]
[ENTER TIM right]
TIM: Please don't be mad! I only came to find my kite.
MARY: Your kite?
TIM: It flew in through the window yesterday.
[HOP TIM]
JOHN: Case closed. The mystery was a kite.

SCENE: beach at sunset
> The next evening, the kite was back in the sky.
TIM: Thank you for helping me!
MARY: Next time, call a detective first.
JOHN: I charge extra for kites.
[HOP JOHN]
